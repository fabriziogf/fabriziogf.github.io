I went into Rockford knowing it wasn't a target race. Five weeks out from Ironman Lake Placid, in the middle of a big training block, the goal was to race clean and come out healthy. No heroics.

I finished in 4:08:01, 4th in my age group and 12th overall. A good race. But I felt flat the whole way, which I expected. More on that below.

A TSS of 419 in one morning is a lot. It's roughly a hard training week squeezed into a few hours.

---

## Swim

22:19 for 1,900m, 1:10/100m average. It was a fast downriver swim, but I was happy with my sighting and technique. The exit was clean and I felt in control through T1, where the focus is to breathe and get blood back into the legs.

## Bike

90.4 km in 2:15:08, 40.1 km/h average, 614m of climbing. Average power 257W, normalized 261W, IF 0.80.

The IF could have been higher (0.83–0.88), but my legs weren't used to that intensity after months of Ironman training. I still set ten personal records on bike segments despite the conservative effort. That's a good sign. **The fitness is there even when I'm not chasing it.**

Cadence averaged 87 rpm, heart rate 132–156 (avg 146). It was 19.7°C with 15–25 mph winds. At times the bike was hard to control, but conditions were good overall.

## Run

21 km (either the course was a bit short or my watch was slow to sync) in 1:25:01, 4:03/km pace, average HR 148, max 160. RPE 9/10.

That RPE is the most honest number of the day. A 9 for 4:03/km means my legs were working harder than they should at that speed. That's the flatness. Fresh, that pace should feel like an 8.

There were good signs too. Cadence was 174 spm, running power was steady (388W avg, 401W normalized), and I didn't fade late. Normalized pace works out to about 4:02/km, which shows the effort held to the finish.

## Why I Felt Flat

Feeling flat at a tune-up race is normal mid-build. I only tapered one week for Rockford. That's not enough to shed five months of progressive load, and I didn't want it to be. A longer taper would have cost training time I need for Lake Placid.

The fitness is there. The difference between today and Lake Placid is two weeks of proper taper: fatigue dropping while fitness holds.

---

## What's Next

Training starts again this week. The plan is to keep building through the week of July 4th, the last big week, then taper properly for two weeks into July 19.

Today confirmed what I needed it to: clean execution, no injuries, fitness in place.

Lake Placid is a different beast, with 2,500m of bike climbing and a full marathon at the end. But the work from January through June is in the legs. Time to see what it adds up to.
