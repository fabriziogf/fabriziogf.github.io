I added a Workout Library tab to the [Training Dashboard](/training/).

It has 90 sessions (30 swim, 30 bike, 30 run) pulled from my actual TrainingPeaks history and laid out as a filterable reference table.

---

## What it is

Every session in the library is one I've done, with a real date, real TSS, and real intervals. No templates. Swims range from 2 to 4.5 km, rides from 1.5 to 6 hours, and runs from 10 to 30 km. Together they cover the full intensity range, from base aerobic work to VO2max.

Each row shows sport, intensity type, training phase, race context, volume, TSS, and the session structure: a short warm-up, a detailed main set (intervals, distances, rest, target zones), and a short cool-down.

## How it's organized

Sessions are sorted by intensity, then by duration within each intensity bucket. The buckets:

- **Base**: A1/Z2 aerobic, equipment sets, progressive builds that start easy
- **Race-pace**: Z3 tempo on the bike, sustained running at goal race speed
- **Threshold**: A2/Z4 work; speed (A3) for swim, FTP-range intervals for bike and run
- **VO2max**: Z5, the uncomfortable ones

Within each bucket, shorter sessions come first.

## Filters

Two filter rows sit at the top of the tab: sport (All / Swim / Bike / Run) and intensity (All Types / Base / Race-pace / Threshold / VO2max). They combine independently: threshold bike only, all runs regardless of type, whatever you need.

## Why

A searchable table is more useful to me than digging through TrainingPeaks for a specific type of session. **The main set descriptions are detailed enough to repeat the workout without opening the original**: specific intervals, rest periods, and zones. That's the point.
