I added a Workout Library tab to the [Training Dashboard](/training/).

It's 90 sessions — 30 swim, 30 bike, 30 run — pulled from my actual TrainingPeaks history and formatted as a filterable reference table.

---

## What it is

Every session in the library is something I've done. A real date, a real TSS, real intervals. Not templates. Swim sessions range from 2 to 4.5 km; bike sessions from 1.5 to 6 hours; runs from 10 to 30 km. Together they cover the full intensity spectrum from base aerobic work to VO2max.

Each row shows sport, intensity type, training phase, race context, volume, TSS, and the session structure: brief warm-up, detailed main set (intervals, distances, rest periods, target zones), brief cool-down.

## How it's organized

Sorted by ascending intensity, then by duration within each intensity bucket. The buckets:

- **Base** — A1/Z2 aerobic, equipment sets, progressive builds starting easy
- **Race-pace** — Z3 tempo bike, sustained running at goal race speed
- **Threshold** — A2/Z4 work; speed (A3) for swim, FTP-range intervals for bike and run
- **VO2max** — Z5, the uncomfortable ones

Within each bucket, shorter sessions come first.

## The filters

Two filter rows at the top of the tab: sport (All / Swim / Bike / Run) and intensity (All Types / Base / Race-pace / Threshold / VO2max). They combine independently — threshold bike only, all run sessions regardless of type, whatever you need.

## Why

Having these in a searchable table is more useful to me than digging through TrainingPeaks to find a specific type of session. The main set descriptions are detailed enough to reproduce the workout without opening the original — specific intervals, rest periods, zones. That's the point.
