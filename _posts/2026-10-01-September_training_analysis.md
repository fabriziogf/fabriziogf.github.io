September was my first month in New York and the month of the Yonkers Marathon. I wrote about the [training plan](/Yonkers_training_plan/) in August, and the [August analysis](/August_training_analysis/) ended with a question: was the plan's race pace of 4:15–4:25 per km too conservative?

For 36 km, yes. I ran 4:09 per km through 36 km. Then the last 6 km fell apart.

The rest of the month was the last two weeks of the build, a taper, and a week and a half of recovery.

## Run

Eleven runs, 13.7 hours, 175 km, including the race.

**The build.** The pattern from August held: threshold on Wednesday, easy running on Friday, and a long run with tempo blocks on Sunday.

- **September 2, 3×2.5 km.** 3:47–4:02 per km, heart rate up to 172.
- **September 6, 25.8 km.** Four tempo blocks between easy running, mostly at 3:50–4:16 per km. Heart rate reached 172 in the last block.
- **September 9, 3×3 km.** The longest repeats of the plan. 3:45–4:01 per km, heart rate 179 by the end of the last one.
- **September 13, 21.1 km.** 2×3 km, 4 km easy, then 2×3 km again. The repeats were at 3:47–4:07, the easy part at 4:40–4:47. Heart rate reached 174 in the last set.
- **September 17, 2×2.5 km.** The last workout, at 3:39–4:06.

Every tempo block was well under the plan's 4:15–4:25. That was the case for racing faster than the plan. I went in aiming for about 2:55, or 4:09 per km.

**Yonkers, September 20.** 3:10:54 on my watch, for 42.63 km with 400 m of climbing.

| Split | Time | Pace |
|-------|------|------|
| 0–10 km | 41:21 | 4:08 |
| 10–21 km | 44:36 | 4:03 |
| 21–30 km | 37:29 | 4:10 |
| 30–36 km | 25:55 | 4:19 |
| 36 km–finish | 41:33 | 6:16 |

Through 36 km I averaged 4:09 per km, right on the 2:55 target. Through 32 km it was 4:07. The hills slowed me in a few places (4:27 on the climb at km 12, 4:32–4:33 at km 33–34), but the pace held.

Then it went. Kilometers 37 to 43 were 4:48, 5:31, 6:01, 6:35, 7:07, 6:56, and 6:46. My legs blew up. I think the cause was hip weakness.

I'm leaving heart rate out of this. It's wrist HR from my watch, and the race trace doesn't hold up: about 171 for the first 27 km, then about 150 from km 28 to 36 at nearly the same pace. I don't trust either number.

**The pace was right for 36 km. My legs weren't ready for 42.** The tempo blocks said I could hold 4:05–4:10, and I did until the legs gave out. That looks like a strength problem more than a fitness problem.

**After.** One run in the last ten days of the month: 7.2 km on September 30 at 5:23 per km, heart rate 129.
